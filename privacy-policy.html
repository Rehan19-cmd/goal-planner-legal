2. Artificial intelligence and your goal data

Goal Planner uses a third-party AI service (OpenAI) to generate your plans and power the in-app coach. This matters, so we want to be plain about it:

  • When you create or regenerate a plan, we send your goal, deadline, stated routine, income band, typed location and any context you wrote to OpenAI so it can produce a plan.
  • When you chat with the AI coach, your messages are sent to OpenAI.
  • We do not send your name, email, phone number, password, payment details, or your community posts to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI processes this data to return a response. Their handling is governed by their own terms and privacy policy.

If you would rather your goal details were never sent to an AI provider, this app is not suitable for you, because plan generation is its core function.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and keep you signed in
  • To generate, store and adjust your plans and daily tasks
  • To track streaks, XP, levels and progress
  • To operate the community feed, connections and profiles
  • To send notifications you have enabled (reminders, streaks, social activity, weekly summaries)
  • To process and verify subscription purchases
  • To detect and fix crashes and performance problems
  • To prevent abuse, spam and fraudulent purchases

5. Other users can see some of your information

If you use the community features, other users can see your name, profile photo, bio, level, streak, badges and anything you post. Stories are visible to other users for 24 hours. Do not post anything you would not want another user to read.

You can set your profile to private, which restricts who can connect with you.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and plan data — kept while your account is active.
  • Stories — automatically deleted 24 hours after posting, including the media file.
  • Crash and analytics data — retained according to the retention periods set by Firebase.
  • Deleted accounts — your data is removed from our active database when you delete your account. Backups may retain it briefly before they cycle out.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form. Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Access tokens are held in the encrypted secure storage of your device. Access to the production database is restricted.

No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
